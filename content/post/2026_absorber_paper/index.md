---
title: Absorber paper published
date: 2026-03-03
---
Members of the research group have been working on a paper the describes the development of microwave absorbers. The paper, led by Dr. Gaganpreet Singh, combines aspects of material design, measurement, and state-of-the art simulations run on High Performance Computing resources availabe to the group through Stockholm University. The paper was published in [Applied Optics](https://opg.optica.org/ao/abstract.cfm?uri=ao-65-8-2603) as Editor's pick and is available on the [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05309) as well.
