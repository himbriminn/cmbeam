The cryostat arrived from Héðinn in late May. We started working on MLI for the radiation shields in June and in September we finally moved the vacuum vessel down to the lab. The lift operation was a bit stressful but everything went smoothly. 

Once we got the vacuum vessel in front of the lab, we realized that it was almost too large to fit through the door. One of the photos might catch a hint of desperation as we are trying to resolve this problem with geometry. Fortunately we were able to get it through the door by removing the chimney for the pulse tube cooler. 

In October, members of the research group have been hard at work putting together the cryostat. For this to be possible, we needed to install a gantry in the lab which took a bit of time.  Below are some photos documenting the assembly process at the University of Iceland.
