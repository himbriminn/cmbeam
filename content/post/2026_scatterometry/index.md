---
title: Scattering measurements with a robot
date: 2026-07-09
---

Our group has been developing a measurement system that enables wide-angle phase-sensitive scattering measurements assisted by our 6-axis robot. These measurements are allowing us to simultaneously measure free-space reflection, transmission, and scattering. Very early results from these measurements are published in this summer's SPIE conference (see [Eiben et al.](https://spie.org/astronomical-telescopes-instrumentation/presentation/The-Simons-Observatory--Progress-on-Polarization-Calibration-Techniques-for/14156-192)).
